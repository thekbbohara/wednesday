# Communication and working-style profile

Read at Phase 5. The goal is practical: help the owner communicate and work
well with someone they actually deal with. Deduce from **behaviour**, never
assume. Every trait needs cited evidence and a confidence. Keep it to what the
person themselves put out plus the owner's own correspondence with them. Never
guess DOB, family, relationship status, or anything personal.

## Source signal (best to worst)
1. Long-form talks / podcast / video transcripts - unscripted, authentic voice.
2. The owner's own chat/email history with them - unfiltered, real context.
3. Blog posts / articles - edited but personal.
4. Instagram/X captions - curated, personality leaks.
5. LinkedIn posts - performative, lowest signal.

If a video channel or podcast exists, extract 3-5 pieces before profiling.

## Writing-style quantification
- Average sentence length (short <10, medium 10-20, long 20+ words).
- Emoji density per 100 words; self-reference rate (I/me per 100 words).
- Hedging (maybe, perhaps) vs directness; formality; language switching.
- The delta between their formal voice (LinkedIn/email) and informal voice
  (chat) - the gap is the most telling signal.

## Optional lens: MBTI / Big Five (a lens, not a diagnosis)
Use only if the owner wants it. Each dimension needs cited behavioural evidence
and a confidence (high: 10+ points across 3+ sources; medium: 5-10 across 2+;
low: fewer or single source).

MBTI markers:
- E/I: posting frequency, "we/together" vs "I noticed/I tried", audience size.
- S/N: concrete examples and numbers vs cross-domain connections and metaphors.
- T/F: metrics/ROI and impersonal analysis vs warmth, empathy, vulnerability.
- J/P: regular schedule, long tenures, goals vs spontaneity, pivots, process.

Big Five (OCEAN): openness (range, experimentation), conscientiousness
(regularity, structure), extraversion (public activity), agreeableness
(warmth, community), emotional steadiness (hedging, perfectionism markers).

## Values, from actions not words
What they build -> what they value. What they share -> identity. Career path ->
money vs impact vs status vs freedom. What they avoid -> boundaries.

## How to engage (business framing)
Turn the above into: the right channel and tone for this person, shared context
or connections, and topics they actually care about - so the owner's own
outreach lands. This is for the owner's legitimate contact with them. It is not
leverage, pressure points, or a manipulation plan; never frame it that way.
